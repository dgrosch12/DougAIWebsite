# Summary — ghostcoded.com

## One-Line Description

A clean, minimalist AI automation services site with a discovery-quiz funnel, strong social proof, and a personal brand focus — built with Vue 3, Tailwind, and PrimeVue.

## Key Design Patterns Worth Replicating

1. **Frosted glass nav bar** — Fixed header with `backdrop-blur-xl` and semi-transparent white background creates a modern, premium feel without being heavy.

2. **Ambient background effects** — Subtle mint-colored blurred blobs + dot pattern grid create depth and visual interest without competing with content. The hero feels alive without any images.

3. **Discovery funnel CTA** — Instead of "Learn More" or "Contact Us", the primary CTA is "Start Discovery" — framing the interaction as personalized guidance rather than a sales pitch.

4. **Staggered review cards** — Review cards animate in with incremental delays, creating a cascade effect that draws the eye across testimonials.

5. **Dark footer contrast** — The jump from white/mint to solid black footer creates a strong visual anchor and makes the footer feel substantial.

## Color Mood

**Clean & Minimal** — Predominantly white with mint/teal accents. The color palette is restrained: black text, white backgrounds, mint (#14b8a6) as the single accent color. The amber star ratings provide the only warm color.

## Overall Vibe/Style

**Modern Professional** — Sits between "startup" and "consultant." The Lexend font gives it a friendly, approachable feel while the minimal color palette and generous whitespace keep it professional. Personal brand energy (Tanner Woodrum) without being overly casual.

## What Makes This Site Effective

- **Simplicity** — Only 3 sections (hero, testimonials, footer). No feature grids, no pricing tables, no complex navigation. Forces focus on the CTA.
- **Social proof placement** — Testimonials are the ONLY content section, making them impossible to miss.
- **Multiple entry points** — Discovery quiz, newsletter, community, direct booking — covers different user intents without cluttering.
- **Typography-driven** — Large, bold headlines with tight tracking create impact without images.
- **Subtle animation** — Floating blobs, staggered card animations, and ripple effects add polish without distraction.

## Direct Applicability to Our Project

- The **frosted glass nav** pattern would work well for a services/AI site
- The **ambient blob background** technique is a modern alternative to hero images
- The **dot pattern overlay** adds texture cheaply and is easy to implement
- The **discovery CTA approach** could be adapted for Douglas AI's service matching
- The **dark/light footer contrast** is a strong closing pattern
- **Review card design** with letter avatars, stars, and staggered animation is a clean social proof pattern
- The single-font strategy (Lexend) keeps things cohesive — consider a similar approach
